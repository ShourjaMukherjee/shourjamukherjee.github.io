Journey

I had to tear away parts of me
Parts that i valued so much
Parts that took so long to build
To reach the moon

When i got here however,
i found nothing but barren land
And the only thing that looked beautiful
Was the earth that i left behind
And the universe that was built around it

My parts now float around in stranded space
And all i can do is helplessly watch
For if i were to reach for them
i would be bound to fail

Maybe some kind floating rock
Will pull them towards itself
And provide them a home for their final moments
As they come tumbling down upon it
And explode into a million more parts
